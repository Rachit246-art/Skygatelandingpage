Frequently Asked Questions

What is the construction cost in Bangalore?
Construction costs typically range from ₹1,800 to ₹3,500 per sq ft depending on the quality of materials and finishes selected.
Do you provide turnkey solutions?
Yes, we provide end-to-end turnkey solutions including planning, approvals, construction, and interiors.
How long does construction take?
A typical luxury villa construction takes between 10 to 14 months from the start of foundation work.
Can designs be customized?
Absolutely. We specialize in bespoke architectural designs tailored specifically to your needs and taste.
Do you assist with approvals?
Yes, our team handles all local body approvals and documentation as part of our turnkey service.
